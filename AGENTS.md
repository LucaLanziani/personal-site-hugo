# Repository Instructions

Before drafting or editing site content, read [WRITING_GUIDELINES.md](WRITING_GUIDELINES.md). It is the canonical guide for voice, editorial direction, and writing style across the site.

Use [docs/content-guide.md](docs/content-guide.md) for content paths, frontmatter, excerpts, assets, and publishing checks. Apply its guidance to the content type being edited.

Before changing site structure, Hugo layouts, or content workflows, read [docs/architecture.md](docs/architecture.md) and the relevant section of [docs/content-guide.md](docs/content-guide.md).

Keep the writing and content guides in sync when changing a publishing convention. The former `WRITING_STYLE_GUIDE.md` has been consolidated into `WRITING_GUIDELINES.md`.
